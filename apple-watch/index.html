PREPARE YOUR DEVICES

Use Apple Watch with your P2S session

Pair and check permissions

Use P2S with your paired iPhone and Apple Watch. Review the Health permissions you want to grant for supported workout and heart-rate features. For an outdoor ruck, check Location access on iPhone as well. Permissions can be changed later in your device settings.

Keep the session aligned

During supported training, P2S shares session details between devices. Synced exercise and rest timers keep the countdown accessible while you work through sets. The Watch gives you a convenient place to review session information without repeatedly reaching for your phone.

Review the completed work

Use your P2S workout and ruck history to review the session afterward. Exercises, sets, reps, weights, and available workout metrics give you a record to compare over time. Apple Health connections can also support saving completed workouts when permission is granted.

Strength sessions and loaded miles

The Watch complements the workout plan you run in P2S on iPhone. Choose a built-in program or your own custom routine, then use the session tools as you train. For rucking, available heart rate adds context to distance, pace, carried load, and elapsed time. Heart-rate availability depends on your devices and permissions.

When a session needs troubleshooting

If information is missing or the devices do not stay aligned, check that the Watch is paired and that the relevant permissions are enabled. Contact P2S Support with your app version, iPhone model and iOS version, plus your Apple Watch model and watchOS version. Include what you tapped and what happened so the issue can be investigated.
