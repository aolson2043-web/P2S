BODYWEIGHT STRENGTH & CAPACITY

Keep training when a gym is out of reach

Build useful strength, muscular endurance, trunk control, conditioning, and mobility using bodyweight training that can be completed in a hotel room or other limited-equipment setting.

Who this program is for

  • Travel
  • Hotel-room training
  • Limited equipment
  • Maintaining training consistency away from home

Use controlled progressions, clean movement, and repeatable effort. The base program requires no exercise equipment.

Equipment and space

No equipment required.

Review the exercises in P2S before your first session so you know which equipment and space each day needs. Built-in programs can be modified to suit your training preferences.

THE WEEKLY STRUCTURE

Know the plan before you start.

Upper- and lower-body strength days build controlled calisthenics. Conditioning and mobility, full-body strength, and bodyweight-capacity work complete the training week. The six scheduled days include an easy recovery day, with a separate rest day. The base program requires no exercise equipment.

  1. Day 01Upper Body Strength
  2. Day 02Lower Body Strength
  3. Day 03Conditioning & Mobility
  4. Day 04Recovery
  5. Day 05Full-Body Strength
  6. Day 06Bodyweight Capacity
  7. Day 07Rest

Session lengths are approximate. Actual duration depends on rest periods, equipment setup, and the work you complete. Follow the day-by-day exercise targets and progression shown in the app.

RUN THE PLAN IN P2S

Make the work visible.

Choose a clear space for your session and review the prescribed movements first. Use controlled repetitions and the program’s progressions. Log completed sets and reps to keep your travel or home sessions in the same training history as your gym work.

P2S keeps exercises, working sets, repetitions, weights and training history together. Built-in timers support rest and timed work, while supported Apple Watch sessions keep useful tools close at hand. You can review completed sessions in your training history and statistics.

Bodyweight Warrior is one of six built-in P2S training paths. The app also includes a custom program builder and standalone ruck tracking. No P2S account is required, and your training records stay on your device.
