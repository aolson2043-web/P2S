Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Prepared to Serve (“P2S,” “we,” or “the app”) is designed to keep your training information under your control. P2S does not require an account, and the app does not send your workout, health, or location records to P2S-operated servers.

Information P2S uses

Training and profile information. The app stores information you enter or create, such as your profile preferences, training programs, custom workouts, exercise logs, body weight, ruck details, and training history. This information is stored locally on your device.

Apple Health. If you grant permission, P2S may read workouts, heart rate, walking and running distance, and active energy from Apple Health to prepare workout summaries and ruck analytics. P2S can also save completed workouts to Apple Health. You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Apple Health information is handled through Apple’s HealthKit framework and is not sent to P2S servers.

Location during outdoor rucks. If you allow location access, P2S uses location while an outdoor ruck is active to calculate distance, pace, and elevation. Background access may be used to keep the active ruck updated when your screen is locked. Location is processed on your device and is not transmitted to P2S servers. Ruck summaries may include the resulting distance and elevation metrics.

Apple Watch. When you use P2S with a paired Apple Watch, session details and workout metrics are shared between your iPhone and Watch using Apple’s device communication features so the session can stay current across both devices.

Purchases. Trial and Pro access are managed with Apple’s StoreKit. Apple processes any purchase. P2S receives purchase entitlement information needed to unlock the app; P2S does not receive or store your payment card details.

How information is used

P2S uses the information described above to provide workout logging, program progress, ruck tracking, statistics, timers, and Apple Watch features. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising or third-party analytics to profile users.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Training records and app preferences remain in the app’s local storage on your device. P2S does not operate a cloud account or upload your training, health, or location records. Apple may process information under its own privacy terms when you use Apple Health, Apple Watch, or the App Store. P2S does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

You can control Health and Location permissions in iOS settings. Data saved to Apple Health can be reviewed or deleted in the Health app. Removing P2S from your device may remove information stored only by P2S; records you saved to Apple Health remain there until you remove them.

Website analytics

The p2sfitness.com website uses Google Analytics to understand page visits, App Store button clicks, and visits from download QR codes. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect browser and device information and approximate location information. This website tracking does not send your in-app workout, Apple Health, or ruck records to Google. For details about how Google processes information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the latest version took effect.
