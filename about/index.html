WHY PREPARED TO SERVE

Why P2S was made.

P2S is for people who train for more than themselves—military members, first responders, and anyone others rely on when it matters.

Created by a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and active first responder.
Built from the perspective of someone who understands that readiness is earned one session at a time.

WHY IT WAS MADE

It started with
a personal need.

The story behind P2S, and the principle that still guides it.

01 / THE ORIGIN

Prepared to Serve began as a tool for myself.

I wanted a workout log I would genuinely use: something dependable that I could take anywhere, that made it simple to record the work, and that documented my progress over time. It needed to be practical, easy to use in real training conditions, and honest about what it recorded.

02 / THE WIDER NEED

Others in similar professions train under similar demands.

As the app took shape, I recognized how closely the need I was solving matched the needs of people in similar lines of work. Military members, law enforcement officers, firefighters, and other first responders hold roles in which physical readiness is part of the job. They need training they can plan, track, and repeat consistently. P2S grew from a personal project into an app built with those professions in mind.

03 / THE PRINCIPLE

One idea set the direction.

Early in development, a single sentence became a guiding principle for the app and for how I think about training: that someone may depend on your fitness on the worst day of their life. Taken seriously, that idea changes what a workout is for. Training is no longer only about personal goals. It becomes part of the commitment to the people who may one day rely on you.

HOW IT SHAPES P2S

A principle
built into the product.

01 / CONSISTENCY

Readiness is built over time

Structured programs, fast workout logging, and built-in timers make it easier to show up, complete the work, and repeat it. Consistency is the goal, and the app is designed to support it without adding friction.

02 / ACCOUNTABILITY

An honest record of the work

Sets, reps, load, distance, time, and pace are recorded as the work happens, so progress is something you can see and verify rather than estimate.

03 / RESPECT

Your training stays yours

No account is required, and training information stays on your device. Health and location access are optional and support only the features you choose to use.
