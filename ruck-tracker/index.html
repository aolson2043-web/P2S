START WITH THE SESSION

How to track a ruck in P2S

1. Choose your session

Open the standalone Ruck Tracker when you want to record a ruck without enrolling in a weekly program. You can also follow ruck sessions within Operational Strength or Forged Operator. Standalone tracking is open-ended, so you decide when the session ends.

2. Set load and terrain

Record the weight you are carrying and select the conditions for your session. Use outdoor tracking for a route outside or treadmill/indoor mode for an indoor effort. A heavier pack and a different surface change the work, even when the distance is similar.

3. Record and review

Start the session and review the resulting record when you finish. Keep actual time, distance, pace, load, and available heart rate together. Outdoor sessions can include elevation; location permission is needed for active outdoor route tracking.

Compare sessions with context

A faster pace is only one part of a ruck. Compare similar distances alongside pack weight, terrain, duration, and available heart rate. A treadmill session and a hilly outdoor route are different conditions; recording those details makes your history more useful. P2S also provides estimated energy figures. These are estimates rather than direct measurements of energy expenditure.

Rucking with iPhone and Apple Watch

A paired Apple Watch supports session information and available heart-rate metrics during supported rucks. Health and Location permissions remain under your control. Tracking depends on available device sensors and permissions; an indoor session does not use an outdoor GPS route. Your P2S training records stay locally on your device.
