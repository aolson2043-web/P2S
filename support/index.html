Common questions

How do I restore my P2S Pro purchase?

Open P2S Settings or the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed into the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Why does P2S ask for Health or Location access?

Health access can provide workout, heart rate, distance, and active energy metrics. Location is used during an active outdoor ruck to calculate distance, pace, and elevation. You can review or change either permission in iOS Settings.

Can I use P2S without creating an account?

Yes. P2S does not require an account. Training information is stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I change a built-in program?

Yes. P2S includes multiple built-in programs that you can modify to suit your preferences and training needs. You can also build or import a custom program. Visit Training to learn more.

Can I track a treadmill ruck?

Yes. Select treadmill/indoor mode so your session summary records the conditions for an indoor ruck. See the Ruck Tracker page for the metrics P2S records.

Does P2S require a subscription?

P2S is free to download. Start seven days of full access by tapping START 7-DAY FREE TRIAL in the app. The trial does not automatically renew or charge you. To continue Pro access afterward, choose the optional $49.99 USD lifetime unlock: a one-time purchase, with no monthly or yearly subscription. Local pricing is shown in the app, and Apple handles the purchase.

Can I log workouts without cellular service?

Yes. Workout logging and saved training history are stored on your device. App Store purchases and restoring purchases may require an internet connection.

Is the name field an account?

No. Your name is a local display name for your athlete profile. No email address or password is required.
